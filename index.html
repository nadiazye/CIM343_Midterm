Space Facts

One million Earths

The Sun is big enough to hold about a million Earths.

Hot Venus

Venus is the hottest planet, even though Mercury is closer to the Sun.

Long years

One year on Neptune lasts about 165 Earth years.

Footprints stay

There is no wind on the Moon, so footprints can last millions of years.

Rain of diamonds?

Scientists think it may rain diamonds deep inside Uranus and Neptune.
